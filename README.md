# Collection of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Mods

This repository contains a curated collection of quality-of-life and gameplay overhaul mods for S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl. Each mod is designed for compatibility and can be used individually or together.

## List of Mods

### Major Overhauls

- **[LongRangeCombat](mods/LongRangeCombat)**: Extends NPC engagement distances, implements a rank-based accuracy model, and optimizes vision/stealth for improved long-range encounters.
- **[RewardingHeadshots](mods/RewardingHeadshots)**: Increases damage dealt to human NPC heads and bodies, rewarding precision gameplay.
- **[StaminaRework](mods/StaminaRework)**: Overhauls stamina management, making hydration and energy drinks more impactful for sprinting and recuperation.
- **[SlowerHealing](mods/SlowerHealing)**: Makes medical and food items heal over longer durations, encouraging a more tactical and cautious playstyle.

### Gameplay & QoL Enhancements

- **[LessSway](mods/LessSway)**: Improves aiming stability by reducing weapon sway by 50% and enabling breath-holding for most scopes.
- **[LessShotgunRecoil](mods/LessShotgunRecoil)**: Reduces the heavy recoil of shotguns for better control during close-quarters combat.
- **[BetterLadder](mods/BetterLadder)**: Improves vertical traversal by increasing ladder climbing speeds and view limits.
- **[FragileCrates](mods/FragileCrates)**: Makes wooden crates and parcels break more realistically with a single hit.
- **[LessWaterSlowdown](mods/LessWaterSlowdown)**: Minimizes movement and turn rate penalties when navigating through water.
- **[LessDeadlyWater](mods/LessDeadlyWater)**: Removes instant death from drowning.
- **[BandagesDontHeal](mods/BandagesDontHeal)**: Focuses bandages on stopping bleeding without providing additional health restoration.
- **[ButtsOfSteel](mods/ButtsOfSteel)**: Removes weapon degradation when using the weapon butt for melee attacks.

### Weapons & Lore

- **[Skif's Holy Pistol](mods/SkifsHolyPistol)**: A specialized version of Skif's Makarov—high precision and power, but prone to jamming if not handled with care.

### Atmosphere & Weather

- **[EternalGloom](mods/EternalGloom)**: Biases the Zone towards perpetual rain, storms, and fog for a more moody and oppressive atmosphere.
- **[SunnierZone](mods/SunnierZone)**: Increases the frequency of clear, sunny weather for those who prefer a brighter Zone.
